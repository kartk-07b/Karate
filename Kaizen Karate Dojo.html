Classes for every age

Each class starts with a warm-up, then technique, then partner practice. We train hard and treat each other with respect.

Balance, listening and basic moves through games. A gentle first step into martial arts.

Adults

Technique, conditioning and self-defence. Train at your own pace, whatever your fitness.
